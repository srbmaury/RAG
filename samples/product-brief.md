# Atlas product launch brief

## Launch decision
The Atlas pilot launches on 15 December 2026 for 20 design partners.
The initial scope includes bulk document uploads, source-backed answers, and private workspaces.
Maya owns the pilot launch. Arjun owns retrieval quality.

## Success measures
The pilot target is 90% citation accuracy on a reviewed evaluation set of 100 questions.
The monthly infrastructure budget is INR 75,000. The budget has not yet been approved.

## Risks and open decisions
Scanned PDFs need OCR before ingestion. This is a risk for customers with image-heavy archives.
The security review must finish before launch. Its completion date is not confirmed.
The final choice of hosting region remains unresolved.
